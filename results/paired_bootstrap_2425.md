# Match bootstrap intervals for the 2024/25 outcome-task comparisons (202 matches, 2,000 resamples)

Generated by `scripts/eval/paired_bootstrap_2425.py`. In each resample the test matches are drawn with replacement, once for all tasks, models and seeds (RandomState 20261003), and AUC is recomputed with the match counts as weights (AUCFast of `scripts/eval/holdout_2526_summarize.py`, ties count one half). Every resample first gives the AUC of each model and seed, then the seed means below, then their difference. Intervals are the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles over the resamples. The share of resamples above zero is given for reference only. Point estimates and intervals are AUC differences.
